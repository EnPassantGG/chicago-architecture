What is this website?

This website is your ultimate guide to exploring 200 of Chicago's most stunning architectural masterpieces. This website organizes these buildings by geographical area, so you can easily find the most notable structures near you. Each entry provides detailed information, photos, and interesting facts, making it simple to discover the city's rich architectural landscape.

What inspired me to create this website?

This website was inspired by my love for Chicago's architecture and the book "Guide to Chicago's Twenty-First-Century Architecture." As someone who has always been captivated by the blend of historic and modern design in the city, I wanted to share that passion with others in a way that’s both engaging and accessible. This site is a way to bring the beauty and diversity of Chicago’s buildings to life, giving everyone a chance to explore the city's architectural wonders on their own terms.
